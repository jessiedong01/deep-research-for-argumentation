\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\sys{} turns a question into a graph of sub-questions, answers it from the leaves upward under generated composition instructions, extends the graph where answers are incomplete, and writes a report that argues for the result. On ten contested motions and in six automated debates, it produced more decisive and more densely cited arguments than OpenAI Deep Research and STORM, at some cost in fluency and with conclusions that were often more qualified than its titles. An automatic attribution test showed, however, that the citations in its evaluated reports did not point to the sources their claims came from, because citation numbers lost their meaning as answers were composed. Assigning each source a single identifier for the whole run removes this failure. A natural next step is to repeat the comparison with a fixed language model and search API, the stance ablations, more motions and raters, and attribution measured for every system.
