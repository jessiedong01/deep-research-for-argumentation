\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\sys{} reduces a question to a sub-question graph and answers it bottom-up under generated composition instructions, grows the graph when answers are not complete, and finally argues the result in a report. It provided more decisive, densely cited arguments than Deep Research and STORM on ten contested motions and six automated debates, but fluency decreased and conclusions were often more qualified than their titles suggested. An automatic attribution test showed that the citations in its evaluated reports do not point to the sources of the claims, since the citation numbers become unintelligible as answers are composed.

Assigning each source a single identifier for the entire run would avoid this failure mode. A controlled comparison on the same language model and search API with the ablations mentioned here, and with more motions and raters, and measuring attribution for all systems is the next step.
